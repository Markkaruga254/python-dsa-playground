# Day 07 — Retrieval practice and weekly review

## Purpose

Today is for consolidation, not for adding another algorithmic pattern. Recalling ideas without looking at notes strengthens the ability to retrieve them later under interview conditions.

## 1. Retrieval before review

Before opening earlier sessions, write down:
- The difference between a list, set, and dictionary.
- The brute-force and hash-map approaches to Two Sum.
- The meaning of O(1), O(n), and O(n²) in the problems you solved.
- The steps in the two-pointer palindrome algorithm.
- The signature used to group anagrams.

Then check your notes and identify gaps. Do not mistake familiarity while reading for independent recall.

## 2. Redo representative problems

Choose three problems that exposed different skills:
1. One basic collection problem (Contains Duplicate or Valid Anagram).
2. One search/hash-map problem (Two Sum).
3. One string/pointer or grouping problem (Valid Palindrome or Group Anagrams).

Start each from a blank file. State the input, output, and assumptions; write a small example; implement; test; then explain time and auxiliary space. Record the hint level honestly using the assessment guide.

## 3. External libraries

The Python standard library provides tested implementations of common operations. Learn to read documentation and decide when a library is appropriate, but do not use a helper to hide the algorithm when the goal is to practise implementing it.

For example, math.sqrt(16) returns 4.0. The result is a float, even though the mathematical value is an integer.

## 4. Review your evidence

Use review.md to record quiz scores, whether you solved each problem, and the highest hint level used. Write one specific topic to revisit. If you cannot explain a solution without reading it, it is not yet mastered.

## Check your understanding

Teach one problem aloud from a blank file. Your explanation should cover the baseline approach, the chosen data structure or pattern, correctness reasoning, complexity, and edge cases.

**Practice:** complete the weekly quiz, redo your hardest problems, and write a realistic focus for Week 2. The solutions/redo.md file is a place to record your independent reattempts.
